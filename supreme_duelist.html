About Supreme Duelist

Supreme Duelist Stickman is a fun and fast-paced stickman fighting game featuring simple controls, physics-based combat, and chaotic battles.

Choose your weapon, enter the arena, and fight against opponents using different weapons and abilities. You can battle against the computer, challenge a friend on the same device, or test how long you can survive in Survival mode.

With a variety of maps, game modes, weapons, and customizable stickmen, every battle can turn into a unique and hilarious fight.
