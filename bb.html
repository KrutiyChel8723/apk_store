About Block Blast

Block Blast is a fun and addictive puzzle game where you place colorful blocks on a grid and try to clear complete rows and columns. The rules are simple, but every move matters, making the game easy to learn and challenging to master.

As you play, you receive different block shapes that must be carefully placed on the board. Complete a row or column to clear it and earn points. Try to create combos and leave enough space for new blocks, because the game ends when you can no longer place any of them.

Block Blast is perfect for quick gaming sessions or longer challenges. With its simple controls, relaxing gameplay, and endless possibilities for improving your high score, it is a great choice for anyone who enjoys classic block puzzles.
